THE STOCKFLOW APPROACH
QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Clear answers.
Before you begin.

Want to explore before creating an account? The demo opens its own isolated workspace.

Can I explore StockFlow without signing up?

Yes. Choose “Explore the live demo” to open a separate demo workspace. Its inventory is isolated from other accounts.

What can I manage in the workspace?

You can create, search, edit and delete products, track quantities, adjust stock with a note, and view dashboard totals.

How does low-stock status work?

Each product can use its own threshold. If it does not have one, StockFlow uses the default threshold for that workspace.

Does each organization see its own inventory?

Yes. Product queries are scoped to the signed-in organization, so one workspace cannot browse another workspace’s products.
